\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Let $f$ be a contraction on a complete metric space with constant
$\rho \in [0, 1)$. Banach's fixed point theorem asserts that the iterates
\begin{equation}\label{eq:banach}
  x^{(k+1)} = f(x^{(k)})
\end{equation}
converge to the unique fixed point $x^\star$.

The bound we will use throughout is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:error}
  \lVert x^{(k)} - x^\star \rVert \leq
  \frac{\rho^{k}}{1 - \rho}\, \lVert x^{(0)} - x^\star \rVert .
\end{equation}

Note that \eqref{eq:banach} is defined in this file, but referenced from the
next one.
