\begin{abstract}
This document exists to exercise \verb|\input| and \verb|\include|: the body
lives in two separate files under \texttt{sections/}, and a label defined in
one is referenced from the other.
\end{abstract}

% Body pulled in from elsewhere. Section numbering, the table of contents and
% cross-references all continue across the file boundary.
\input{sections/intro}
\include{sections/method}

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Multi-file builds are a supported input, not an afterthought: see
\S\ref{sec:method} for the estimate and \S\ref{sec:conclusion} for this section.
